# Interview (runtime-neutral)

Canonical: `.claude/commands/interview.md` and `.claude/skills/job-application-assistant/07-interview-prep.md`

Build a stage-specific prep pack from the tracked application archive: the posting, the CV and letter actually submitted, and recorded feedback.

Research the company and interviewers with verify-before-use. Map likely questions to STAR examples already in the profile. Offer a mock interview if asked.

Gaps get honest bridge answers. Never invent experience.
